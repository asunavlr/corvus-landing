Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Corvus is a program that runs on your machine. This policy covers two different things: this website and the program. They work differently, so they are treated separately here.

1. The Corvus program

Corvus is a local process. It starts in your terminal, listens on localhost:3210, and keeps everything in a SQLite database inside your own user folder (~/.corvus/corvus.db, or wherever CORVUS_HOME points).

What we do not collect:

  • Your code, your files and the contents of your folders.
  • Your conversations, prompts, answers and agent transcripts.
  • Folder paths, project names and settings.
  • Telemetry, usage statistics, crash reports or machine identifiers.

There is no user account in the program, no license key and no online activation. We have no server receiving anything from your Corvus, because the program sends us nothing.

Where your data does go:

  • Anthropic. Corvus runs the Claude Code CLI already installed on your machine. Everything you say to the agent goes to Anthropic, under the account and terms you already have with them. The same would happen from a terminal.
  • Sites you ask it to verify. When an agent opens a browser to check a screen, it visits the address you gave it, with the credentials you supplied.
  • MCP servers and tools you already configured. Corvus inherits your machine's environment. Whatever you had enabled stays enabled, with the same reach as before.

In all of these cases the relationship is directly between you and the third party. We do not mediate or see that traffic.

2. This website

This page is static. No forms, no login, no signup, no analytics, and it stores no cookies.

What does exist:

  • Your language choice is saved in your browser's localStorage, on your machine. It never leaves it.
  • Google Fonts. The page loads typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. That makes your browser contact Google, which receives your IP address and user agent, under their own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosting. The site is served by Vercel, which keeps its own infrastructure access logs.

3. Payment

The subscription happens entirely on GitHub Sponsors. We do not receive, process or store any payment data — no card numbers, no billing address. What reaches us is what GitHub shows any sponsored account: the username of the sponsor, so read access to the repository can be granted. The data you give GitHub is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

4. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data of yours, there is no database of ours to access, correct or erase. If you still wish to exercise any right under the Brazilian LGPD (Law 13.709/2018) or the GDPR, write to the address below and we will answer.

The data Corvus itself stores lives on your machine and is under your control: deleting the database file deletes all of it.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced on this page.

6. Who is responsible, and contact

Corvus is maintained by Kevin Matos, an individual — not a company. For anything concerning this policy, including the rights in section 4, write to editorial@agropujante.com.br.
